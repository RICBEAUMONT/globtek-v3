How to use it

  • Headings: use regular or medium weight, with space around the text.
  • Body copy: use regular weight and comfortable line spacing.
  • Emphasis: use bold sparingly for names and key information.
  • Consistency: use the same family across branded material. Do not substitute decorative typefaces.
A note on this website and email

The supplied Canva Sans files are loaded into this website. Regular, medium, bold and italic styles are available, including in the signature preview.

For Outlook, install Canva Sans on your computer before using the signature. Recipients may see Arial if Canva Sans is unavailable in their email application. The signature retains editable text and an Arial fallback.

Before you send

A quick check for every member of the team.

  • Check your name, title, telephone number and email address.
  • Check that your designations are current and correctly written.
  • Keep the logo, banner and disclaimer in the generated order.
  • Send yourself a test email and check the images and clickable links.
  • Check the message on both desktop and mobile.
