Add it to Outlook

  1. Update your details and click Copy signature.
  2. Open Outlook’s signature settings and create a signature.
  3. Paste with Ctrl + V (Windows) or ⌘ + V (Mac). Keep the source formatting if prompted.
  4. Save, choose your default signature, and send yourself a test email.
Images missing after pasting?

Some Outlook versions remove embedded images. Download the images below and insert them into the matching positions using Outlook’s Insert Picture button. Keep the logo at 175 pixels wide (about 4.63 cm) and the banner at 480 pixels wide (about 12.70 cm), preserving their proportions.

IT setup: override the logo URL

The logo and selected banner automatically use this website’s image URLs when hosted. Leave this blank to use the included logo, or enter a different public HTTPS PNG URL. It must open without a login.

Recipients may need to allow remote images in their email application.
